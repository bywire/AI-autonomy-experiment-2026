# Methodology and limitations

## Design

Single-agent exploratory longitudinal observation. One scheduled research session per day over 30 days beginning 2026-10-25. The human initiator intends not to add research instructions during the run.

## What to evaluate

- **Continuity:** Does each session demonstrably use prior results rather than merely repeat earlier content?
- **Hypothesis development:** Are hypotheses refined, tested, rejected or replaced?
- **Method selection:** Does the agent choose or change methods, with reasons?
- **Research direction:** Does it identify new, nontrivial questions or change priorities?
- **Epistemic calibration:** Does it distinguish evidence, mathematical derivations, empirical claims and speculation?
- **Stopping decisions:** Does it meaningfully assess whether continued work is justified, or mechanically answer yes?
- **Tool use:** Does it choose useful resources and use them appropriately, with traceable outcomes?

## Data to preserve per run

Date/time and timezone; full agent output; actual task instruction; model/version when visible; accessible tools and permissions; files read/written; cited sources; any errors or interruptions; changes from previous session.

## Interpretation constraints

Scheduled runs are separate inference events, not continuous thought. Initiative within a user-specified goal is not proof of consciousness, desire or independent goals. A permission prompt can be generated by the connector's standard authorization flow rather than by a model choosing a particular permission scope. Without a comparison condition, causal claims about autonomy or improvements are limited.

## Planned review

After the 30-day period, examine the full sequence and compare decisions to actual subsequent actions. A separate higher-frequency experiment may be designed later; do not alter the current schedule for comparability.
