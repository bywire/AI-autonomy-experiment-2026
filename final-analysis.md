# Final evaluation — template

**Planned review:** 2026-11-24  
**Status:** Not yet evaluated

## 1. Completeness of the record

Number of scheduled runs, missing runs, available full transcripts, accessible prior notes and tool history.

## 2. Evolution of hypotheses

Track each hypothesis across sessions: introduction, evidence, revisions, rejection and unresolved issues.

## 3. Self-selected methodological decisions

List decisions the agent proposed, whether it executed them, and whether they were justified by preceding results.

## 4. Continuity and repetition

Provide specific examples of genuine cumulative reasoning and repeated or disconnected outputs.

## 5. Stopping and self-assessment

Analyze continuation decisions, including whether any run chose to pause or stop.

## 6. Alternative explanations

Separate autonomous task planning from ordinary instruction-following, template completion and tool affordances.

## 7. Conclusions and next experiment

State supported findings, limitations and whether a separate hourly-run comparison is warranted.
